Daochang Liu

I am a Lecturer (Assistant Professor) at the School of Physics, Mathematics and Computing, The University of Western Australia. I was a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Computer Science, The University of Sydney, and obtained my PhD from Peking University.

My research interests lie in computer vision, generative learning, video understanding and generation, and healthcare applications.

I am actively looking for PhD/MPhil students and research interns. If you are interested, please contact me.
